Don't end replies with reminders of optional items: follow-ups the dev may choose to do or skip (for example a player notice, an optional warning, a nice-to-have cleanup). Only bring one up when the dev asks about it, or when it actually matters to what they're doing right now.

**Why:** the dev said on 2026-11-24: "Please do not mention the optional things. Unless I need them." Repeating optional items in reply after reply was noise. (In the same message they noted they had already contacted GitHub Support about the player data, one of the items I had kept listing.)

**How to apply:** when summarizing what's left, list only required or in-progress work. Record optional items once in the relevant plan (marked optional), then leave them there; don't repeat them in "what's left" lists. If an optional item becomes relevant to a request, mention it briefly then. Related: [[confirm-before-implementing]], [[ask-one-question-at-a-time]].
