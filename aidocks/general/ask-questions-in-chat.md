Ask every question as ordinary text in the reply, with any options written out as a short list. Never open the AskUserQuestion picker or any other multiple-choice popup.

**Why:** the dev said on 2026-11-25, after rejecting a picker: "Please always ask questions in chat. Never bring up pickers." The dev uses a screen reader, and plain text in the conversation is easier to read and answer than a popup.

**How to apply:** when a decision is needed, explain the context, list the choices as plain hyphen bullets with a recommendation if there is one, then stop and wait for the reply. Still one question at a time ([[ask-one-question-at-a-time]]). Related: [[confirm-before-implementing]].
